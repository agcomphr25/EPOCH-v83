FOD Prevention (Foreign Object Debris)
4

📌 TRAINING PURPOSE (Read Aloud)

This facility standard defines Foreign Object Debris (FOD) prevention practices required to protect product integrity, flight safety, and compliance in an aerospace composite manufacturing environment.

FOD prevention is everyone’s responsibility.

1️⃣ What FOD Is & Why It Matters
What FOD Means
FOD (Foreign Object Debris) is any object that does not belong in or on a product, tool, mold, or work area.

Examples include:

Loose hardware

Tools

Rags

Tape backing

Gloves

Hair, jewelry, or personal items

Carbon or fiberglass debris

Why FOD Is Critical

FOD can:

Cause internal defects

Create structural weaknesses

Lead to in-service failures

Result in product escapes and recalls

🔴 IMPORTANT POINTS
FOD can be invisible once enclosed

Small objects can cause major failures

Composite parts can trap FOD permanently

Prevention is mandatory

2️⃣ FOD Control Areas & Expectations
Controlled Areas
FOD control applies to:

Cutting tables

Layup areas

Mold assembly/disassembly

CNC

Finish

QC / Shipping

Entry Expectations

Before entering controlled areas:

Secure loose items

Empty pockets if required

Wear approved clothing

Remove jewelry and accessories

🔴 IMPORTANT POINTS
Loose items are prohibited

Personal items increase risk

Entry checks prevent defects

Everyone is accountable

3️⃣ Tool Control & Accountability
Tool Control Requirements
Use only approved tools

Keep tools organized

Account for tools before and after tasks

Return tools to designated locations

Tool Accountability

Missing tools must be reported immediately

Never assume a tool is “somewhere else”

Tool shadow boards must be followed

🔴 IMPORTANT POINTS
Unaccounted tools = FOD risk

Tool tracking is mandatory

Never leave tools unattended

Missing tools stop work

4️⃣ Housekeeping & Work Area Cleanliness
Clean-As-You-Go Standard
Keep work areas clean throughout the task

Remove scrap and debris promptly

Do not allow buildup of dust or fibers

End-of-Task Cleanliness

Inspect work area

Inspect tools and molds

Dispose of waste correctly

🔴 IMPORTANT POINTS
Cleanliness is a quality requirement

Dust and fibers are FOD

Messy areas hide defects

End-of-task inspections are required

5️⃣ Materials, Consumables & Packaging
Consumable Control
Control peel ply, breather, tape, backing paper

Dispose of excess materials immediately

Do not reuse single-use materials unless approved

Packaging Awareness

Remove packaging completely

Inspect materials before use

Prevent packaging fragments from entering work areas

🔴 IMPORTANT POINTS
Backing paper is common FOD

Consumables must be controlled

Packaging must not enter parts

Inspect before use

6️⃣ Personal Responsibility & Behavior
Personal FOD Sources
Jewelry

Watches

Rings

Earbuds

Pens or markers

Behavior Expectations

Secure personal items

Report FOD immediately

Correct unsafe behavior respectfully

🔴 IMPORTANT POINTS
Personal items are FOD risks

Convenience does not override safety

Peer reminders are professional

Silence allows defects

7️⃣ FOD Inspections & Verification
When Inspections Occur
Before starting work

During critical steps

Before closing parts or molds

At task completion

Inspection Responsibilities

Inspect parts, molds, and tools

Verify cleanliness

Document inspections as required

🔴 IMPORTANT POINTS
Final inspections catch hidden FOD

Documentation proves compliance

Never skip inspections

Inspection is part of the job

8️⃣ Stop-Work Authority (FOD)
When to Stop Work
Stop work immediately if:

FOD is suspected

A tool or item is missing

Cleanliness cannot be verified

A foreign object cannot be accounted for

🔴 IMPORTANT POINTS
Anyone may stop work

No permission required

FOD concerns override schedule

Work resumes only when resolved

9️⃣ FOD Culture & Continuous Improvement
Cultural Expectations
Prevent, not react

Speak up early

Improve processes

Share lessons learned

Improvement Mindset

Identify recurring FOD risks

Suggest improvements

Fix root causes

🔴 IMPORTANT POINTS
FOD prevention is continuous

Lessons learned prevent repeats

Process improvements matter

Everyone contributes
