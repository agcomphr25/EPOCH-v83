PTO OPERATIONAL CONSTITUTION — EPOCH

Objective:
Establish the official operational policy, ownership model, approval authority, payroll governance, audit expectations, and exception handling standards for PTO inside EPOCH.

This document defines how PTO must function operationally across the company.

This is not a software task.

This is the business + compliance constitution that the software must enforce.

This should be written as a formal internal operational standard suitable for:
- ownership review
- HR enforcement
- payroll enforcement
- supervisor accountability
- DCAA audit defensibility
- employee handbook alignment

Business Context:
EPOCH is the system of record for PTO requests, approvals, payroll readiness, and leave auditability.

PTO must be:
- operationally simple
- payroll-safe
- supervisor-accountable
- audit-defensible
- government contractor compliant
- resistant to informal “just handle it” exceptions

Historical behavior included:
- owners verbally approving leave
- receipts, texts, hallway approvals
- post-it note requests
- after-the-fact payroll adjustments
- undocumented exceptions

This is no longer acceptable.

EPOCH is now the required workflow.

Required Constitution Sections:

==================================================
SECTION 1 — POLICY STATEMENT
==================================================

Define:

- PTO requests must be submitted in EPOCH
- verbal approval is not approval
- text message approval is not approval
- handwritten note approval is not approval
- payroll cannot process undocumented PTO
- EPOCH is the authoritative source of truth

Clear executive statement required.
