Step 6 — AI Routing / Traveler Requirements Wizard
Purpose
This step decides what P2 must create before production can start.
The AI should generate a recommendation, but the user must approve it.

Step 6 Wizard Flow
1. User selects or confirms part/process type
Fields:
Part Type:- Composite- CNC Machined- Assembly- Sub-Assembly- Paint / Finish- Special Process- Shipping / Final Inspection OnlyProduction Type:- New Part- Repeat Part- Revision Change- First Article- Rework- Prototype

2. AI reviews WAD/project inputs
AI uses:
Part numberPart nameCustomerQuantityRevisionRisk assessment resultDepartments requiredMaterial typeSpecial process requirementsPrior routing exists? yes/noPrior traveler exists? yes/noCustomer/spec requirementsInspection requirements

3. AI generates production-control requirements
Recommended output:
{  routingRequired: true,  travelerRequired: true,  workInstructionRequired: true,  specSheetRequired: true,  finalQcOnlyAllowed: false,  inProcessInspectionRequired: true,  spotCheckPlanRequired: true,  certRequired: false,  reason: "Composite production requires controlled routing, traceability, traveler signoffs, in-process inspection, and final QC."}
