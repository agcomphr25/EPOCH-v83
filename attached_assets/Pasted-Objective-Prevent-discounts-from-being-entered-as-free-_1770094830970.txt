Objective:
Prevent discounts from being entered as free text and ensure that any discount affecting order totals
must exist in structured discount fields.

Context:
We identified cases (e.g., EI193) where a discount appears in Sales Order PDF text or notes
but is not applied to totals because it was never stored in structured discount fields.

This is NOT a calculation bug.
This is a data-contract and UX enforcement issue.

We will enforce the rule:
"If a discount affects price, it must be represented in structured discount fields."

---

PART 1 — Enforce Discount Discipline (No Free-Text Discounts)

Rules:
1. Free-text fields (customer notes, internal notes, feature descriptions) must NEVER affect pricing.
2. Only structured fields may affect pricing:
   - Persistent discounts (discountCode)
   - Custom discounts (showCustomDiscount + customDiscountType + customDiscountValue)
3. Pricing logic must remain unchanged.

Implementation:
- Do NOT parse discounts out of notes or feature text.
- Do NOT attempt to auto-convert text into discounts.
- Leave resolveOrderPricingSummary() exactly as-is.

---

PART 2 — Add CSR UX Nudge (Soft Warning, Not a Hard Block)

Goal:
Prevent CSRs from accidentally typing discounts into notes and assuming totals will update.

Add a UI warning when ALL of the following are true:
- Notes or feature descriptions contain discount-like language
  (examples: "discount", "% off", "$ off", "price reduction")
- AND no structured discount is active:
  - discountCode is null/none
  - AND showCustomDiscount is false or customDiscountValue is null

Warning Behavior:
- Display a non-blocking warning message near the notes field
- Do NOT block saving the order
- Do NOT auto-apply any discounts

Suggested Warning Copy (exact wording optional):
"⚠️ This order mentions a discount in notes, but no pricing discount is applied.
Notes do not affect totals. Use the Discount section to apply pricing discounts."

---

PART 3 — Visual Confirmation When Discounts ARE Applied

When a structured discount IS active:
- Display a small confirmation indicator near totals:
  Example:
  "Discount Applied: GB-20 (20%)"
  or
  "Custom Discount: $57.90"

Purpose:
- Reinforce correct workflow
- Reduce CSR confusion
- Make pricing intent obvious before PDFs are generated

---

PART 4 — Safety & Constraints

Constraints:
- Do NOT modify existing order data
- Do NOT backfill historical orders
- Do NOT mutate snapshots
- Do NOT change pricing math
- Do NOT add regex-based discount parsing

This is a UX and data-discipline change only.

---

Acceptance Criteria:
- A discount typed only in notes does NOT affect totals
- CSRs receive a visible warning when this occurs
- Structured discounts continue to work exactly as before
- Sales Order PDFs always match calculated totals
- This class of mismatch cannot occur silently again

Deliverables:
- Files modified
- Screenshot or description of the warning UI
- Confirmation that pricing logic was not altered
