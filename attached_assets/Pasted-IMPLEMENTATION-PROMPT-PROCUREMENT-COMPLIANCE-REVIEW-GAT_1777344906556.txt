SECTION 1 — COMPLIANCE REVIEW UI
==================================================

When user clicks:

Issue PO

system opens:

PROCUREMENT COMPLIANCE REVIEW

Required review fields:

--------------------------------------------------

GOVERNMENT / FLOWDOWN REVIEW

1. Does this PO support a government contract?

Options:
- Yes
- No

2. Are FAR/DFARS clauses required?

Options:
- Required
- Not Required

3. Is DPAS rating required?

Options:
- Required
- Not Required

4. Is Certificate of Conformance required?

Options:
- Required
- Not Required

5. Is MTR / traceability required?

Options:
- Required
- Not Required

6. Is source inspection required?

Options:
- Required
- Not Required

7. Is second-party approval complete?

Options:
- Yes
- No

8. Vendor approved for this work?

Options:
- Yes
- No

--------------------------------------------------

JUSTIFICATION

Required free-text field:

Compliance Review Notes

Example:

“Customer PO includes FAR flowed-down clauses and MTR requirement for serialized aerospace material.”

This is mandatory.
