EPOCH DOMAIN TRUTH INSPECTOR — SYSTEM FORENSIC AUDIT
I want you to perform a FULL READ-ONLY FORENSIC ARCHITECTURE AUDIT of the EPOCH codebase in order to design a "Domain Truth Inspector".

The Domain Truth Inspector will live at:

/admin/domain-truth

Its purpose is to allow administrators to inspect the true state of any object in the system and understand why it appears (or does not appear) in specific workflows, department queues, or dashboards.

Examples:
• Why an order is not appearing in the Finish queue
• Why a PO receipt did not trigger an inventory event
• Why a production order did not advance departments
• Why an order skipped sequence numbers

DO NOT modify any code.
DO NOT implement anything.
ONLY perform a full architecture investigation.

Node
Express

Postgres

all_orders
