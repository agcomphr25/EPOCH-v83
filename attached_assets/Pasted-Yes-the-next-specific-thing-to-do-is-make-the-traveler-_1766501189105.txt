4) Tighten the traveler–routing link (what “tighter” means)

“Tighter” doesn’t mean more foreign keys.

It means:

traveler stores routing revision used

traveler stores snapshot OR has generated immutable steps/tasks that reflect that revision

edits to templates do not retroactively change existing travelers

That’s the key difference between a “software workflow” and a “quality record”.
