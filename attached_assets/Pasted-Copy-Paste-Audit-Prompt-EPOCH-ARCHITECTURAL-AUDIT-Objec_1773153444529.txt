Objective: Identify how Parts, Products, Materials, and BOM structures currently exist in the codebase and determine the safest path toward a unified "Item Master" architecture.

• READ ONLY — do not modify any files

CONTEXT
EPOCH is a manufacturing ERP with multiple production lines:

P1 — Stocks
P2 — Aerospace

Both production lines may manufacture items, purchase materials, and eventually sell finished goods.

We are preparing to implement Quotes, Sales Orders, and Invoices.

However the current system may separate:
- parts
- products
- materials
- assemblies

Best practice ERP architecture uses a single unified ITEM MASTER that can support:

purchase
sales
manufacturing
inventory
BOM

The goal of this audit is to determine:

1) How items are currently represented
2) Where the system distinguishes between parts vs products
3) How BOMs reference parent and child items
4) Whether manufacturing parents are invoiceable
5) Whether products are linked to inventory
6) Whether P1 and P2 have different item logic
