# Labor Charging Policy

**Effective date:** 2026-05-06
**Owner:** Controller
**Change summary (initial):** Initial draft establishing direct vs. indirect labor charging rules and WAD authorization requirements.

## Purpose

Define how labor hours must be charged to ensure costs are accumulated against the correct cost objective (project, work order, or indirect pool) in accordance with FAR 31 and DCAA expectations.

## Scope

Applies to all labor hours recorded in EPOCH, regardless of employee classification.

## Policy

1. **Authorized charge only** — Hours may only be charged to an active charge code or to a Work Authorization Document (WAD) the employee is authorized to perform.
2. **Direct labor** — Hours that benefit a specific final cost objective must be charged directly to that work order or project. Direct labor must never be charged to an indirect pool.
3. **Indirect labor** — Overhead, fringe, G&A, and other indirect activities must be charged to the appropriate indirect charge code; never to a direct work order.
4. **No cross-charging** — Hours worked on Project A may not be charged to Project B for any reason, including budget overruns or favorable rates.
5. **WAD enforcement** — The system records every labor capture against the active WAD; budget overruns or unauthorized work are flagged for supervisor review (Phase 1 = warn; Phase 2 = block).
6. **Unallowable activities** — See [unallowable-costs.md](./unallowable-costs.md). Time spent on unallowable activities must be charged to the designated unallowable indirect code.

## Roles & responsibilities

- **Employees:** Select the correct charge code or WAD before starting work; raise discrepancies immediately.
- **Project managers:** Maintain accurate WAD scope and budget.
- **Controller:** Owns the charge code chart and indirect pool definitions.
