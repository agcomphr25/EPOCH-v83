What & Why

Right now, labor can be viewed in the PM Control Center, but it is not yet fully controlled by WAD + traveler context.

This ticket enforces that:

All direct labor must be tied to a valid WAD charge code
Charge codes are automatically assigned based on:
project
work order
traveler step (department/task)
Labor cannot continue when:
budgeted hours are exhausted (configurable: warn vs hard stop)

“tracking time”
