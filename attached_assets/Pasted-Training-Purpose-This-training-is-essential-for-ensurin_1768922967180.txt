Training Purpose
This training is essential for ensuring employees understand the critical role of travelers in AS9100 manufacturing environments. Travelers:
Serve as the official manufacturing record for a product.
Control how parts are built, inspected, documented, and accepted.
🔴 IMPORTANT POINTS:
No traveler = no authorized work.
Travelers ensure processes are performed correctly and traceably.
---
1️⃣ What a Traveler Is
#### Definition A traveler is an authorized, controlled document that accompanies a part or batch through every production step. It defines:
Required process steps.
The order of operations.
Inspection and verification points.
Documentation and signatures required.
Material and traceability requirements.
#### What the Traveler Is NOT
A suggestion.
A memory aid.
Optional paperwork.
Something you “fill out later.”
🔴 IMPORTANT POINTS:
The traveler is the process.
Work must follow the traveler exactly.
If it’s not on the traveler, it’s not authorized.
The traveler always overrides memory or habit.
---
2️⃣ Why Travelers Are Required by AS9100
#### AS9100 Expectations AS9100 requires organizations to:
Plan and control production.
Ensure repeatability of processes.
Provide objective evidence of conformity.
Maintain full traceability.
#### Why Auditors Care Auditors use travelers to verify:
Correct processes were followed.
Required inspections occurred.
Materials were approved and traceable.
Work was performed by authorized personnel.
Records were completed in real-time.
🔴 IMPORTANT POINTS:
Auditors trust travelers, not verbal explanations.
Missing traveler steps = nonconformance.
Incomplete travelers = failed control.
Travelers are legal quality records.
---
3️⃣ What the Traveler Controls
#### Process Control The traveler specifies:
Each operation.
Required tools or equipment.
Process parameters.
Sequence of steps.
#### Quality Control The traveler defines:
Inspection points.
Hold points.
Required measurements.
Acceptance criteria.
The traveler also captures:
Material lot numbers.
Batch numbers.
Expiration dates.
Serial or job numbers.
🔴 IMPORTANT POINTS:
Sequence matters.
Skipping ahead is prohibited.
Traceability must be recorded exactly.
Inspection steps are mandatory.
---
4️⃣ Using the Traveler Correctly
#### Before Starting Work
Verify the correct traveler.
Confirm revision and job number.
Review all upcoming steps.
Ensure required materials and tools are available.
#### During Work
Perform steps in order.
Record data as work is performed.
Stop if instructions are unclear.
Do not proceed past hold points.
#### After Completing a Step
Verify work meets requirements.
Sign or authenticate only after completion.
Do not pre-sign or back-date.
🔴 IMPORTANT POINTS:
Read before doing.
Record in real-time.
Never work ahead of the traveler.
Never sign before work is complete.
---
5️⃣ Signatures, Initials & Accountability
#### What a Signature Means A signature or digital sign-off indicates:
You performed the work.
You followed the traveler.
The work meets requirements.
The recorded information is accurate.
#### Signature Rules
Only sign for your own work.
Shared logins are prohibited.
Signing is a legal attestation.
🔴 IMPORTANT POINTS:
A signature is a legal commitment.
Never sign for someone else.
Never allow someone else to sign for you.
Signatures create accountability.
