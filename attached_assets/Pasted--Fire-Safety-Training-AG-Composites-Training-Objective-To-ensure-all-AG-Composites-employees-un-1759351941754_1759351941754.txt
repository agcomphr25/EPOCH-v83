🔥 Fire Safety Training – AG Composites
Training Objective

To ensure all AG Composites employees understand fire safety hazards, prevention measures, emergency response, and evacuation procedures in a composites manufacturing environment. Employees will be able to recognize risks, respond appropriately, and follow company protocols to maintain a safe workplace.

1. Fire Hazards in Composite Manufacturing

Flammable Materials

Epoxy resins, hardeners, and solvents (acetone, alcohol).

Carbon fiber and fiberglass dust (highly combustible).

Paints, primers, adhesives stored in finishing areas.

Heat & Ignition Sources

Ovens and curing equipment.

Grinding, cutting, CNC machines producing sparks.

Improperly discarded rags soaked in solvents.

Electrical Hazards

Overloaded circuits.

Faulty extension cords or exposed wiring.

Improper grounding of equipment.

2. Fire Prevention

Housekeeping

Keep exits, walkways, and fire extinguishers clear.

Dispose of solvent-soaked rags in approved fire-safe containers.

Regularly sweep and vacuum carbon/fiberglass dust (use explosion-proof vacuums).

Storage

Store chemicals in flammable safety cabinets.

Label all containers properly.

Keep incompatible chemicals separated (e.g., oxidizers vs solvents).

Equipment

Inspect electrical cords and tools regularly.

Ensure ovens/curing equipment are properly calibrated and shut down after use.

Employee Conduct

No smoking in or near production areas.

Do not use open flames (torches, lighters) in restricted areas.

3. Fire Emergency Procedures

Raise the Alarm

Activate nearest fire alarm pull station.

Notify supervisor and dial 911 immediately.

Evacuation

Stop work immediately.

Follow posted evacuation routes.

Do not run—move quickly and calmly to designated assembly area.

Supervisors will account for all employees.

Containment (If Safe)

Small incipient fires may be fought with an extinguisher only if trained and safe to do so.

Never attempt to fight a large or spreading fire.

Do NOT

Do not re-enter the building until cleared by Fire Department or Safety Officer.

4. Fire Extinguishers

Types Used at AG Composites

Class A – wood, paper, trash.

Class B – flammable liquids (resins, solvents, paints).

Class C – electrical equipment.

PASS Method

Pull the pin.

Aim at the base of the fire.

Squeeze the handle.

Sweep side to side.

5. Critical Safety Points

Always know the location of nearest exits and fire extinguishers.

Never block fire exits with materials or equipment.

Report frayed cords, overheating machines, or chemical leaks immediately.

Participate in quarterly fire drills—treat every drill as real.

Remember: Your safety is the priority—equipment and materials can be replaced.
