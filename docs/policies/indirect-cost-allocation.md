# Indirect Cost Allocation Policy

**Effective date:** 2026-05-06
**Owner:** Controller
**Change summary (initial):** Initial draft defining indirect cost pools, allocation bases, and rate computation.

## Purpose

Establish the indirect cost pools, allocation bases, and methodology used to allocate indirect costs to final cost objectives in accordance with FAR 31.203 and DCAA expectations.

## Scope

All indirect cost pools maintained in the EPOCH chart of accounts.

## Policy

1. **Pool structure** — The company maintains separate indirect cost pools for: Fringe Benefits, Manufacturing Overhead, G&A, and Unallowable. Each pool has a documented composition and an allocation base.
2. **Allocation bases** —
   - Fringe: applied as a percentage of total direct + indirect labor dollars.
   - Manufacturing Overhead: applied as a percentage of direct manufacturing labor dollars.
   - G&A: applied as a percentage of total cost input.
   - Unallowable costs are accumulated but never allocated to final cost objectives.
3. **Rate computation** — Indirect rates are computed monthly using actual costs and bases. Provisional rates may be used in-period; year-end true-up reconciles to actual.
4. **Pool integrity** — A cost may belong to exactly one pool. Reclassification between pools requires controller approval and produces an audit event.
5. **Documentation** — Each pool's composition and base is documented and reviewed annually.
