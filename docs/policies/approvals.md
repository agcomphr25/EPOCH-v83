# Approvals Policy

**Effective date:** 2026-05-06
**Owner:** Director of Operations
**Change summary (initial):** Initial draft defining multi-stage approval responsibilities.

## Purpose

Define who approves what, on what cadence, and what evidence the system retains to support DCAA review.

## Scope

Time entries, PTO requests, labor cost allocations, timesheet corrections, and payroll exports.

## Policy

1. **Self-certification** — Every employee certifies their own daily time before it can be approved.
2. **Supervisor approval** — Each employee's direct supervisor approves time weekly. Approval is per-employee, per-period; bulk approval is permitted but each row must be traceable.
3. **Three-stage PTO** — PTO requests follow a three-stage approval flow: requester → supervisor → operations / HR. Supervisors only see their own team's requests.
4. **Labor cost approvals** — Cost-bearing labor allocations require supervisor approval before posting to GL.
5. **Payroll export approval** — A payroll export batch requires (a) all included employees fully certified, (b) all included supervisors fully approved, and (c) explicit "release" by the payroll administrator.
6. **Segregation of duties** — A user may not approve their own time, PTO, or correction.

## Roles & responsibilities

- **Supervisors:** Daily exception review, weekly batch approval.
- **Operations / HR:** PTO final stage, leave balance reconciliation.
- **Payroll administrators:** Pre-export verification and release.
