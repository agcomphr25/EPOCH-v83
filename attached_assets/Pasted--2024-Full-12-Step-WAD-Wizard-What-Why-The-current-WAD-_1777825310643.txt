#2024 - Full 12-Step WAD Wizard
What & Why
The current WAD is auto-created from a quote and only captures basic fields (part number, budget hours, department budgets as raw JSON). The spec calls for a structured 12-step wizard that collects the full work authorization record: scope, department work breakdown, DCAA-compliant charge codes per department, material authorization rules, routing/traveler requirements, quality requirements, schedule, risk controls, required documents, and multi-role approvals. This turns the WAD from a thin seed record into the authoritative authorization document that drives travelers, time-clock enforcement, and audit traceability.

Done looks like
A dedicated WAD creation/edit wizard with 12 clearly labeled steps and a progress indicator
Step 1 auto-populates contract context from the linked project and PO (project number, customer, PO number, quote, part/rev, quantity, ship date, contract review status, risk assessment status); PO Review must be Approved before a WAD can be started
Step 2 captures scope of work: what is authorized to build, quantity, build type (Prototype / Production / Repair / Engineering Sample), departments involved (multi-select from the updated department list), and deliverables
Step 3 generates a work breakdown table by department — each row has department, operation, responsible lead, estimated hours, required certs, and flags for traveler step and QC signoff required
Step 4 captures DCAA-critical charge codes per operation: charge code, labor category, direct/indirect/unallowable classification, budgeted hours, overtime allowed, operator override allowed, and overrun rule (warn / require approval / hard stop)
Step 5 defines material/BOM authorization rules: BOM linked, material lots required, serialized material, ICN scan, expiration blocking, out-time tracking, customer-supplied material, certs required
Step 6 defines routing and traveler requirements: routing required, traveler required, work instruction required, spec sheet required, in-process inspection required, final QC only, spot check plan
Step 7 captures quality requirements: inspection level, FAI required, in-process QC, final QC, spot check details (sample size, frequency, acceptance criteria, escalation rule), customer source inspection, cert package, dimensional report, NCR process
Step 8 defines schedule and capacity: authorized start date, required completion date, per-department due dates, priority, daily target qty, capacity risk, bottleneck department
Step 9 carries over risks from the risk assessment: technical, schedule, material, quality, tooling, supplier risks; ITAR flag; customer flow-downs; special process controls — each with owner, mitigation, due date, approval status
Step 10 is a document checklist: attach or confirm/waive customer PO, drawing, rev/spec, work instructions, BOM, routing, quote, risk assessment, purchase review checklist, material cert requirements, inspection plan, flow-downs; required docs must be attached or waived before WAD approval
Step 11 collects approvals: project manager, production manager, quality, finance/accounting (optional: engineering, compliance/export control); each records user, role, timestamp, decision, comments
Step 12 is a final review screen showing all gate items as ✅/❌ before setting WAD status to APPROVED. Final WAD approval does not release the project to P2 by itself — it only satisfies the WAD gate. P2 release still requires PO Review Approved + WAD Approved + Preproduction Complete (handled by Task #2023).
The department enum is updated to: CUTTING_KITTING, LAYUP, CURE, CNC, SUB_ASSEMBLY, ASSEMBLY, FINISH, PAINT, QC, SHIPPING
Each department entry supports an isSpecialProcess and requiresCertification flag for future cert enforcement and vendor/OSP routing
