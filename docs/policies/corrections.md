# Timesheet Corrections Policy

**Effective date:** 2026-05-06
**Owner:** Payroll Administrator
**Change summary (initial):** Initial draft codifying the after-the-fact timesheet correction workflow.

## Purpose

Provide a controlled, auditable mechanism to correct time entries discovered to be inaccurate after they were originally recorded, while preserving DCAA total-time-accounting integrity.

## Scope

Applies to any change to a previously certified or approved time entry, regardless of pay period status.

## Policy

1. **Correction-only edits** — Once an entry is certified, the original value is never overwritten. All changes go through the correction workflow, which produces a paired before/after record.
2. **Reason required** — Every correction must include a written reason describing what was wrong and why the change is needed.
3. **Approval required** — The employee's supervisor approves the correction. Corrections affecting closed pay periods additionally require payroll administrator approval.
4. **Closed-period block** — Corrections to a pay period that has an active payroll export batch are blocked until the batch is finalized or voided (see [period-close.md](./period-close.md)).
5. **Notification** — The affected employee is notified of any correction made on their behalf.
6. **Audit trail** — All corrections, including the original value, new value, reason, requester, approver, and timestamps, are stored immutably.

## Roles & responsibilities

- **Employees:** Submit correction requests promptly upon discovering an error.
- **Supervisors:** Review reason and approve / reject within 2 business days.
- **Payroll administrators:** Re-validate totals and re-export payroll if a closed period is reopened.
