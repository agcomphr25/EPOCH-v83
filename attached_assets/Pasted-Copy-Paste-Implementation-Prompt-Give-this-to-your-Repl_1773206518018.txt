PHASE 2 — Aggregation Job

Create service:

server/services/modelStatsAggregator.ts

Function:

rebuildModelDepartmentStats()

Algorithm:

SELECT
model_id,
department,
AVG(duration_minutes),
PERCENTILE_CONT(0.5),
COUNT(*)
FROM order_department_transitions
JOIN all_orders
ON entity_id = all_orders.id
GROUP BY model_id, department

Insert results into model_department_stats.

Rules:

ignore samples < 5 orders
fallback to department averages when insufficient data
