The dashboard must NEVER require manual updates.

We are NOT building scheduling.
We are NOT assigning today’s 22 tubes in advance.
We are NOT building approvals from the dashboard.

We ARE building:

DAILY TUBE THROUGHPUT BOARD

Where:

- Slot #1 = first tube whose Layup Started event occurs today
- Slot #2 = second tube started today
- Slot #3 = third tube started today
- etc.

This continues until approximately 22 daily production slots.

The board shows:

“How many tubes entered production today, where are they now, and what is preventing completion?”

The board resets daily.

A “green” slot means:

The tube successfully reached the final required TODAY milestone
(before shift end)

—not necessarily full completion.

If a traveler is blocked/held/rework/etc.,
the board should visibly reflect that.

The board itself performs NO actions.

It is visibility only.
