IMPLEMENTATION PROMPT — PROCUREMENT COMPLIANCE EFFECTIVE DATE + LEGACY SEGMENTATION

STRICT COMPLIANCE-FIRST EXECUTION

DO NOT weaken the Procurement Compliance Review Gate.
DO NOT reduce FAR_FLOWDOWN integrity.
DO NOT fake historical compliance reviews.
DO NOT silently grandfather legacy POs.
DO NOT manipulate ERDI scores without policy-backed logic.

Goal:

Create a formal Procurement Compliance Effective Date system so Vendor POs issued before formal enforcement are treated as legacy transactions, while all POs after the effective date remain under mandatory compliance enforcement.

This is policy-backed governance.

Not score manipulation.

==================================================
OBJECTIVE
==================================================

Current problem:

The rewritten Procurement ERDI scorer now correctly evaluates real compliance evidence.

However, many historical Vendor POs were issued before the Compliance Review Gate existed and therefore:

- have no compliance review
- have no FAR applicability documentation
- have no second-party review evidence
- were issued under legacy controls

These should not be falsely treated as if they failed a process that did not yet exist.

We need:

PROCUREMENT COMPLIANCE EFFECTIVE DATE

and

LEGACY PO SEGMENTATION

==================================================
POLICY MODEL
==================================================

Two populations must exist:

--------------------------------------------------
1. LEGACY POPULATION
--------------------------------------------------

Vendor POs issued BEFORE:

Formal Compliance Effective Date

Example:

June 1, 2026

These are:

Legacy Procurement Transactions

They remain governed by prior controls and should not be treated as failing the new Compliance Review Gate by default.

They may still be reviewed if:

- audit-sensitive
- government contract critical
- legal exposure exists
- still-open compliance obligations exist

But they should not poison standard ERDI enforcement scoring.

--------------------------------------------------
2. ENFORCED POPULATION
--------------------------------------------------

Vendor POs issued ON or AFTER:

Formal Compliance Effective Date

These MUST satisfy:

- compliance review
- FAR applicability decision
- required optional statements
- second-party approval
- vendor approval
- audit evidence
- issue gate enforcement

No exceptions.

Create a controlled setting:

Procurement Compliance Effective Date

admin-controlled

auditable

effective_date

This matters.

==================================================
PART B — ERDI SCORER SEGMENTATION
==================================================

Update Procurement ERDI scoring:

FAR_FLOWDOWN
SECOND_PARTY_APPROVAL
VENDOR_APPROVAL_BLOCKING
REQUISITION_WORKFLOW
etc.

Primary scoring population:

issued Vendor POs >= effective_date

Legacy POs:

issued Vendor POs < effective_date

must be excluded from mandatory failure scoring
unless specifically flagged for exception review

Scorer must distinguish:

“non-compliant”
vs
“legacy pre-policy transaction”

This is critical.
