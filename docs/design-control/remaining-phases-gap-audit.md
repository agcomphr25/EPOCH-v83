## Release decision

PR #1651 must remain draft and **DO NOT MERGE** while any row above is `MISSING`, `SERVER ONLY`, or `EXISTS BUT INCOMPLETE` for a required acceptance criterion. In particular, structured record lifecycle integration, the operational traceability matrix, complete final-review aggregation, PostgreSQL 16.4 certification, and authenticated browser certification remain blockers to describing all remaining Design Control phases as complete.

This audit deliberately preserves the authority boundary: the R&D project owns the engineering process; Design Control orchestrates; BOM, routing, traveler, work-instruction, inspection, tooling, document-control, and P2 modules remain authoritative for their own records.
