My recommended simplified version for EPOCH now:
DRAFTIN_REVIEWAPPROVEDRELEASEDACTIVECLOSED

Best EPOCH Rule
The WAD should not create the traveler.
The WAD should authorize the work.
P2 should create the routing and traveler.
Project/WAD = authorizationP2 = executionTraveler = shop-floor recordTime Clock = labor record
That separation is the cleanest for AS9100 + DCAA.
