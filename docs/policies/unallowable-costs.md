# Unallowable Costs Policy

**Effective date:** 2026-05-06
**Owner:** Controller
**Change summary (initial):** Initial draft establishing identification, segregation, and exclusion of FAR Part 31 unallowable costs.

## Purpose

Ensure that costs determined to be unallowable under FAR Part 31 are identified, segregated, and excluded from any billing, claim, or proposal to the federal government.

## Scope

All costs incurred by the company, including labor, materials, services, and indirect costs.

## Policy

1. **Identification** — Costs identified as unallowable per FAR 31.205 (e.g., entertainment, alcohol, lobbying, certain advertising, fines, bad debts) are marked unallowable at the time of recording.
2. **Segregation** — Unallowable costs are charged to the dedicated Unallowable indirect pool / charge codes. They are accumulated for completeness (per FAR 31.201-6) but are excluded from the allocation bases used to allocate other indirect costs.
3. **Directly associated costs** — Costs associated with an unallowable activity (e.g., travel costs for an unallowable trip) are also unallowable and segregated.
4. **Training** — All employees who incur or approve costs receive annual training on identifying unallowable costs.
5. **Disclosure** — Unallowable cost balances are reported in the annual incurred cost submission.
6. **Audit trail** — Reclassification of a cost between allowable and unallowable requires controller approval and produces an audit event.
