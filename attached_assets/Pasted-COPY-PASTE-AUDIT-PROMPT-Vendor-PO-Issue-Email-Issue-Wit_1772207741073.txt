PART 3 — Safety Requirements for “Issue Without Email”
------------------------------------------------------------

We REQUIRE an explicit “Issue Without Email” path for legacy re-entry.
But it must be hard to do accidentally and it must be auditable.

Audit what exists today and assess whether it meets these requirements:

1) Explicit label:
Must say “Internal only — do NOT notify vendor”

2) Confirm friction:
At least one of:
- checkbox “I confirm I do not want to notify the vendor”
- typed confirmation (e.g. type ISSUE)
- required reason field (recommended)

3) Audit trail:
A durable record with:
- who performed it (user id/email)
- timestamp
- vendor_po_id / po_number
- reason (if provided)
- a flag like issued_without_email = true

4) UI indicator:
After issuing without email, the PO page must display:
“Vendor NOT notified.”
